WHAT ARE COOKIES?
================================================================================

Cookies are small files stored on your computer that help websites remember
information about you. Used for:
  • Keeping you logged in
  • Remembering preferences
  • Analyzing website usage
  • Showing relevant content

================================================================================
TYPES OF COOKIES ON MANAWA
================================================================================

1️⃣ ESSENTIAL COOKIES (Always Active - No Consent Needed)

These cookies are REQUIRED for MANAWA to function properly.

   Cookie Name        Purpose                      Duration
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   session_id         Keeps you logged in         Until logout
   csrf_token         Security (prevents attacks) Session
   user_preferences   Language/theme choice       1 year
   payment_status     Payment processing          Session
   auth_token         JWT authentication          7 days

WHY ESSENTIAL?
  • Without session_id: You'd need to login on every page
  • Without csrf_token: Attackers could hijack your account
  • Without payment_status: Payments would fail


2️⃣ ANALYTICS COOKIES (Requires Your Consent)

These track how you use MANAWA to help us improve.

   Cookie Name        Tool                Duration
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   _ga                Google Analytics    2 years
   _gid               Google Analytics    24 hours
   amplitude_id       Amplitude Analytics 10 years

WHAT WE LEARN:
  ✓ Which pages you visit
  ✓ How long you stay
  ✓ What you click on
  ✓ Where you came from

NOTE: No personal data shared. Only anonymous statistics.


3️⃣ MARKETING COOKIES (Requires Your Consent)

These help us show you relevant ads and measure effectiveness.

   Cookie Name        Tool                Duration
   ────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   fbp                Facebook Pixel      90 days
   gclid              Google Ads          Session
   utm_source         Our tracking        Session

HOW WE USE IT:
  • Show ads for MANAWA on Facebook/Google
  • Measure if ads work
  • Retarget users who visited but didn't book


4️⃣ FUNCTIONAL COOKIES (Requires Your Consent)

These enhance your experience with additional features.

   Cookie Name              Purpose                 Duration
   ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
   notification_pref        Your notification pref  1 year
   recent_searches          Your search history     30 days
   favorites                Your bookmarked pros    1 year

================================================================================

When you first visit MANAWA, you see:

   ┌────────────────────────────────────────────┐

   │  [Accept All] [Reject All] [Settings]     │

WHAT EACH BUTTON DOES:

Accept All:
  ✅ Essential      (always)
  ✅ Analytics      (yes)
  ✅ Marketing      (yes)
  ✅ Functional     (yes)

Reject All:
  ✅ Essential      (always)
  ❌ Analytics      (no)
  ❌ Marketing      (no)
  ❌ Functional     (no)

Settings:
  • Choose which types to allow
  • Different per category

YOUR RIGHTS
================================================================================

YOU CAN:
  ✅ Change consent anytime (click "Cookie Settings")
  ✅ View what cookies we store
  ✅ Request deletion of tracking cookies
  ✅ Use MANAWA without analytics (service still works)

WE CANNOT:
  ❌ Delete essential cookies (site won't work without them)
  ❌ Stop tracking if you accept analytics
  ❌ Honor opt-out if you accept marketing cookies
