QUERY PARAMETERS:

  - ?limit=N - Items per page (1-500)
  - ?sort=field1,field2 - Sort fields (comma-separated)
  - ?order=asc,desc - Sort directions (comma-separated)

FIELD NAME PATTERN:
  - Allowed: /^[a-zA-Z_][a-zA-Z0-9_]*$/
  - Example valid: id, createdAt, _count, field_name
  - Example invalid: 1field, field-name, field$, id;DROP
