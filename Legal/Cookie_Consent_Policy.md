# 🍪 COOKIE CONSENT POLICY — MANAWA

**Effective Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Applies to:** All website visitors

## WHAT ARE COOKIES?

Cookies are small files stored on your computer that help websites remember information about you. They can be used for:
- Keeping you logged in
- Remembering preferences
- Analyzing how you use the site
- Showing relevant ads

---

## TYPES OF COOKIES ON MANAWA

### **1️⃣ ESSENTIAL COOKIES (Always Active)**

These cookies are REQUIRED for MANAWA to function. We do NOT need your consent for these.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `session_id` | Keeps you logged in | Until logout |
| `csrf_token` | Security (prevents attacks) | Session |
| `user_preferences` | Your language/theme choice | 1 year |
| `payment_status` | Payment processing | Session |
| `auth_token` | JWT authentication | 7 days |

**Why essential?**
- Without `session_id`, you'd need to login on every page
- Without `csrf_token`, attackers could hijack your account
- Without `payment_status`, payments would fail

---

### **2️⃣ ANALYTICS COOKIES (Requires Consent)**

These track how you use MANAWA to help us improve the experience.

| Cookie | Purpose | Tool | Duration |
|--------|---------|------|----------|
| `_ga` | Google Analytics tracking | Google Analytics | 2 years |
| `_gid` | Session tracking | Google Analytics | 24 hours |
| `amplitude_id` | User analytics | Amplitude | 10 years |

**What we learn:**
- Which pages you visit
- How long you stay
- What you click on
- Where you came from

**No personal data is shared.** We only see anonymous statistics.

---

### **3️⃣ MARKETING COOKIES (Requires Consent)**

These help us show you relevant content and ads.

| Cookie | Purpose | Tool | Duration |
|--------|---------|------|----------|
| `fbp` | Facebook tracking | Facebook Pixel | 90 days |
| `gclid` | Google Ads tracking | Google Ads | Session |
| `utm_source` | Marketing source | Our tracking | Session |

**How we use it:**
- Show you ads for MANAWA on Facebook/Google
- Measure if ads work
- Retarget users who visited but didn't book

---

### **4️⃣ FUNCTIONAL COOKIES (Requires Consent)**

These enhance your experience with additional features.

| Cookie | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|----------|
| `notification_pref` | Your notification settings | 1 year |
| `recent_searches` | Your search history | 30 days |
| `favorites` | Your bookmarked professionals | 1 year |

## YOUR RIGHTS

✅ **You can:**
- Change your consent anytime (click "Cookie Settings")
- View what cookies we store
- Request deletion of tracking cookies
- Use MANAWA without analytics cookies (service still works)

❌ **We cannot:**
- Delete essential cookies (site won't work)
- Stop tracking analytics if you accept them
- Honor opt-out requests if you accept marketing cookies

## MANAGING COOKIES

### In Browser Settings:
- **Chrome:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies
- **Firefox:** Preferences → Privacy → Cookies

- **Edge:** Settings → Privacy → Cookies

### At MANAWA:
- Click "Cookie Settings" in footer
- Toggle each category on/off
- Click "Save Preferences"
- Refresh page
